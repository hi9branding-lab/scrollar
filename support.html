An app says "Restricted" after my quiz

Open Scrollar, go to Settings, and choose your locked apps again. If it keeps happening, email us the app name and the time.

I did not get the quiz notification

Turn on notifications for Scrollar in iOS Settings. Scrollar needs them to start a quiz.

How do I change the apps I lock?

Open Scrollar, tap the gear icon, then tap Apps to lock.

How do I change my course or level?

Open Scrollar, tap the gear icon, then change Studying for and Level.

Is my data safe?

Scrollar has no account and collects no data. Read the Privacy Policy.
