Scrollar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Scrollar helps you practice English before you open apps that distract you. We built it to work without collecting your data.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. Scrollar has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no server.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your settings, such as your course, level, and daily goal
  • Your quiz progress, streak, and the questions you missed
  • The apps you choose to lock

This information stays on your device. Your device backups may include it, under your own iCloud or computer settings. Delete the app to remove it from your iPhone.

Screen Time

Scrollar uses Apple's Screen Time tools to lock the apps you choose. iOS gives Scrollar private tokens for those apps, not a list of what you use. This information never leaves your device.

Notifications

Scrollar sends notifications from your device to start a quiz and, if you turn it on, to remind you to practice. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

Translation and voice

Explanations can be translated with Apple's Translation feature, and questions can be read aloud with voices on your iPhone. Scrollar does not send this text to us. Apple's own privacy terms apply to Apple's features.

Children

Scrollar does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
