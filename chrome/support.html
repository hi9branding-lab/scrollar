Scrollar for Chrome support

Email 111amyle@gmail.com. Include your Chrome version, extension version, and the steps that caused the problem. Do not include passwords or private URLs.

A site is not locked

Open Scrollar, turn Quiz lock on, and add the domain in Settings → Sites to lock. Allow Chrome’s access request. Confirm the extension is enabled in this Chrome profile. Other profiles, browsers, native apps, and Chrome internal pages are outside its scope.

A timer did not relock immediately

Chrome can delay alarms while the computer sleeps or is busy. Scrollar checks for expiry when its background worker starts and periodically while Chrome is running. Use Lock now to relock a site manually.

Where are the notes?

Choose an answer, then hover or focus an option to see its note. Select a translation language in Settings → Translate to.

Where is my iPhone progress?

The Chrome extension stores progress separately in this browser profile. It does not sync with the iPhone app.

Remove data or a site

Use Reset progress in Settings to clear learning progress. Remove selected sites to stop protecting them and remove their permissions. Uninstalling removes the extension’s local data.
